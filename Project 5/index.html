Healthy Plate

Building a Balanced Meal

A balanced plate gives your body the nutrients it needs to grow tall, run fast, and stay alert.

Aim to fill half your plate with colorful vegetables and fruits, one quarter with grains like brown rice or whole-wheat pasta, and the final quarter with protein like beans, paneer, lentils, or eggs.

Learn More at MyPlate.gov

Smart Everyday Snacks

Smart snacking keeps your energy steady throughout the day without crashing.

Reach for sliced apples with peanut butter, roasted chickpeas, or crunchy cucumber sticks instead of packaged chips and sugary sodas.
